HOW TO PLAY

Chess, with one
beautiful complication.

All the familiar rules still hold. Then a capture changes the physics of the board.

01

Captures transfer power

When your piece captures, it inherits the captured piece's movement. If a knight takes a queen, that knight now moves like a queen. If a queen takes a pawn, it is reduced to a pawn's stride.

× →
02

The crown is permanent

A king stays royal no matter which power it captures. It can become a queen on legs — but it is still the piece that must be protected.

03

Everything else is chess

Check, checkmate and stalemate remain. Castling works while your king still has king power and your rook still has rook power.

04

Pawns still promote

Any piece moving with pawn power that reaches the far rank becomes a queen. Capture first, then promotion applies.

05

Optional tilt

On supported phones and tablets, tap the tilt button to lean the board with your device. iOS asks for motion access first — nothing is read until you allow it.
